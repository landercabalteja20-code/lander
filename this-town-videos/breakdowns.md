## 01 · Everyone watches these videos (promo)

The format turned on itself: the rule is that everyone in town watches "In this town" videos. It ends by pitching the creator's course. Sunny pastel city throughout.

| Time | Beat | What's said | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:07 | Hook | "In this town, everyone is watching these *in this town* videos." Then "…when I say everyone, I mean everyone." | Aerial down a pastel main street, then people on their phones |
| 0:07–0:12 | Rule in action ×3 | Watching while eating breakfast, walking the dog, even on the toilet | One literal shot per example |
| 0:12–0:30 | Proof character 1 | The teacher watches one in class ("the floor turns into lava every hour") and writes LAVA instead of math | Classroom, a video-within-the-video of a lava floor, chalkboard reading "LAVA" |
| 0:30–0:46 | Proof character 2 | Grandma watches "everyone has a mammoth" and tries to order a baby mammoth online | Giant mammoth in the street, Grandma browsing a "BABY MAMMOTH" listing |
| 0:46–1:00 | The problem | "How are these videos even made?" Wrong guesses (a studio, years of 3D). The best friend tries: his character walks through a wall and his computer crashes | Neon studio grid, desk buried in books, a brick wall, an "ERROR / CRASHED" screen |
| 1:00–1:12 | Turn | "Everyone loves watching these videos, but so many people have no idea how to create them." | Hero walking through the town |
| 1:12–1:39 | Pitch | The academy and what it teaches, "even if you've never animated anything before" | Academy building, classroom with STORY / AI / ANIMATION signs |
| 1:39–1:48 | CTA + callback | "link in my profile", then "…the next Roblox story everyone in this town is watching will be yours." | "LINK IN PROFILE" sign, aerial of the town plaza |

**Technique to steal:** even a sales pitch is told as a town story with the same beats: rule, three examples, proof characters, a problem, a solution.

## 02 · Paid for being unlucky (twist + moral flip)

A rule that rewards bad luck, and a hero with perfect luck. Sunny pastel city, with a teal "Bad Luck Center".

| Time | Beat | What's said | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:03 | Hook | "In this town, you get paid for being unlucky." | Aerial down the main street |
| 0:03–0:11 | Rule in action ×3 | Missed bus $100 → rain $500 → house destroyed $1,000,000 | In-world "+$100" money pop-ups, a rain cloud over one person, a money explosion |
| 0:11–0:24 | Proof characters | Neighbor struck by lightning three times now owns a mansion. The richest man's jet crashed into his pool: "$50,000,000!" | Neighbor under a storm cloud with floating money labels; the rich man in a top hat by his pool, a jet splash, money raining down |
| 0:25–0:30 | "But me?" | "My name is Milo, and I have a huge problem. I've never been unlucky." | Milo (light-blue hoodie) facing camera |
| 0:31–0:55 | Attempts 1–2 | Misses the bus, but a limo gives him a free ride: "$0." Stands in a thunderstorm, but the rain stops and a rainbow appears: "$0 again." | Bus, limousine, porch in the rain, rainbow |
| 0:55–1:09 | Attempt 3 | Buys a lottery ticket hoping to lose, wins the jackpot, but the prize gets locked away: "I was still broke." | Lottery booth, money locked in a vault |
| 1:09–1:17 | Contrast character | Best friend drops his phone in a toilet, gets "$10,000!", buys a sports car. "I'd had enough." | "+$10,000" pop-up in the bathroom |
| 1:17–1:28 | Authority confirms it | Bad Luck Center face scan. "Sir, you have 0% bad luck." | Teal building, giant screen "BAD LUCK: 0%" |
| 1:28–1:47 | Last try | A bus famous for breaking down starts smoking ("Finally!"), but a mechanic fixes it in 10 seconds | Smoking bus, mechanic, driver handing over a pass |
| 1:47–1:58 | Low point | "I sat on my bed and whispered, why can't one bad thing happen to me?" | Sunset bedroom, sad face close-up |
| 1:58–2:14 | Twist | A phone message: officially "the unluckiest person in history", because "having perfect luck is the worst luck of all." | POV of the phone screen, the message revealed line by line |
| 2:14–2:25 | Payoff + callback | "$1 billion", "the richest person in town", "happy to be unlucky" | "+$1,000,000,000" money rain, happy porch shot |

**Techniques to steal:** a repeating punchline ("$0" → "$0 again"); the hero's strength becomes the problem, and the twist turns the problem into the win; the final line echoes the hook.

## 03 · Monster that moves in the dark (cliffhanger)

The rule is a threat, and the story is a countdown of failures. Night palette with floodlights, then rain.

| Time | Beat | What's said | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:08 | Hook + stakes | "In this town, there's a monster that can only move in the dark." Then, if the lights go out for one second, "it's over for all of us." | Night aerial of the plaza: the monster ringed by floodlight towers |
| 0:09–0:21 | Backstory | Appeared three years ago; seven people disappeared; it froze at sunrise; its weakness is light | Crowd at night, the monster frozen in daylight |
| 0:21–0:31 | The system (numbers) | Six floodlights, a backup generator each, 12 guards with flashlights | Floodlight towers, generators, guards |
| 0:31–0:40 | Inciting line + hero | "…hasn't moved an inch, until last night." Then "My dad is the head of security…" | Hero (yellow jacket) with Dad (navy uniform) |
| 0:40–1:05 | Chain of failures | 2:13 AM, "Boom!", a floodlight explodes. "Relax, we've still got five." The radio reports the main cable is damaged, then "Sir, they're overheating." | Exploding light with sparks, radio close-ups, Dad's face |
| 1:05–1:24 | Everyone helps | Police headlights, guards' flashlights, lamps in windows. "Then, click." The whole town goes dark except the monster | Police cars, beams crossing on the monster |
| 1:24–1:45 | Complications | Thunder and rain, a guard slips, headlights die, the hero trips and his flashlight flies. The monster is in the dark for one second | Hero trips over a cable, the flashlight flies, the monster's silhouette in the dark rain |
| 1:45–2:02 | Twist | A scream; the lights come back. It has moved, and it's holding a guard, "still alive" | Monster right in front of them |
| 2:02–2:12 | Cliffhanger | "Dad, we have to save him!" "Don't move!" "Why?" "Because if those lights go out again, he's dead." | Dad grabbing the hero's arm, the guard's shocked face |

**Techniques to steal:** specific numbers make the world feel real (six lights, 12 guards, 2:13 AM); radio dialogue moves the plot with no narration; each fix fails one step at a time; the last line is dialogue that reframes the danger.

## 04 · Gold pill or black pill (viewer vote)

A choice rule with a mystery, told in present tense so the audience can decide part 2. It moves from smoggy slums to a purple-and-gold neon ceremony hall.

| Time | Beat | What's said | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:14 | Hook + options | Everyone chooses between two pills on their 16th birthday: gold makes you rich for life, black "reveals the biggest secret in our city", and "there's no going back" | Aerial of a smoggy street, then a ceremony hall full of kids, glowing pills |
| 0:14–0:31 | Hero + contrast | "My name is Lucas, and tomorrow, I turn 16." Poor district below, floating mansions above | Slums, then floating islands with pools |
| 0:31–0:45 | Emotional stakes | Mom: "promise me you'll choose gold." His sister disappeared on her 16th birthday; "I never believed them." | Mom's face close-up, a dark alley at night |
| 0:46–1:15 | The ceremony | Limo and guards, the Pill Selection Center. His best friend takes gold: suit, "New balance, $10 million!", a golden elevator | Neon stage, giant screen "NEW BALANCE: $10,000,000!", confetti, "LUXURY DISTRICT" doors |
| 1:15–1:28 | Hero's turn | The official explains both pills. "I reach for gold." | Official in white and gold, pills on glowing rings |
| 1:29–1:46 | Twist | A note slides under a locked door: "Lucas, don't take gold. Trust me, Ruby." "That's my missing sister!" | "RESTRICTED" door, POV of the handwritten note |
| 1:45–1:55 | Dilemma + timer | One pill saves the family, the other might find Ruby. "Make your choice!" Ten seconds left | Red countdown "00:10 MAKE YOUR CHOICE" |
| 1:56–2:05 | Vote CTA | "What should I do, guys?… Vote in the poll below… that's the one I'll take in part two." | Hero between the two pills, countdown at 00:01 |

**Techniques to steal:** both options must feel right (money for family vs. finding the sister); a real countdown on screen; present tense; turning the cliffhanger into a comment and poll prompt sets up part 2.
