# "In This Town" script template

Target 320–400 words, which runs about 2:00. **One line is one shot.** Keep sentences around 7 words. See [`README.md`](README.md) for the full style spec.

## Fill-in template

```
HOOK  (0:00–0:04)                                  [shot: aerial over the town]
In this town, ______________________________.
And if ______________, ______________.                 ← stakes (optional)

RULE IN ACTION  (0:04–0:25)                        [shots: street-level, one per line]
______________? $___.                                   ← example 1 (small)
______________? $_____.                                 ← example 2 (bigger)
And if ______________? $__________!                     ← example 3 (absurd)
That's why ______________.
My neighbor ______________. Now he ______________.      ← proof character 1
And ______________? ______________.                     ← proof character 2

"BUT ME?"  (~0:25)                                  [shot: medium, hero facing camera]
But me? My name is ______, and ______________.
______________.                                         ← the problem in one line

ESCALATION  (0:30–1:30)                             [3 mini-stories, 3–5 lines each]
One morning, ______________.
I thought ______________.
But then, ______________.
______________.                                         ← punchline (set up a pattern)

Another day, ______________.
… ______________.                                       ← same punchline again ("… again.")

I even ______________.
But somehow, ______________.

Meanwhile, my best friend ______________.               ← contrast character
So I visited ______________.                            ← official place / authority
"______________."                                       ← dialogue that confirms the problem

LOW POINT  (~1:35)                                  [shot: sad close-up]
I went home ______________.
I sat on my bed and whispered, ______________?

TWIST  (~1:45)                                      [shot: POV of a phone / note / screen]
Suddenly, ______________.
It said, "______________."
I froze.

ENDING  (last 5–15 s), pick one:
A) Moral flip: In a world where ______, ______ is ______.  → payoff → callback to "this town"
B) Cliffhanger: one short line of dialogue that changes everything.
C) Vote: What should I do, guys? Should I ______ or ______? Vote in the poll below.
         Whichever gets the most votes, that's what I'll do in part two.
D) Promo: ______. You can find the link in my profile.
```

## Example script (original): "Gravity turns off"

About 335 words, so roughly 2:00 at the references' pace. It uses ending B (cliffhanger), with ending C (vote) as an alternative below.

| # | Line (voiceover) | Shot |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | In this town, gravity turns off for one minute every day. | Aerial over the town at noon, clock tower in the center |
| 2 | And if you're not holding onto something, you float away. Forever. | A baseball cap drifting up past the rooftops |
| 3 | At 11:59, the whole town grabs on. | Street-level wide: people rushing to lampposts and railings |
| 4 | Kids hug lampposts. | Two kids hugging a lamppost, eyes squeezed shut |
| 5 | Bus drivers buckle in. | Close-up: a driver clicking a seatbelt |
| 6 | My grandpa ties himself to his chair, then ties the chair to the house. | Grandpa on the porch, rope everywhere |
| 7 | Even the dogs wear tiny seatbelts. | A dog in a seatbelt harness, looking smug |
| 8 | Then, at 12:00, everything lifts. | Giant screen flips to 12:00; small objects start rising |
| 9 | Cars. Coffee cups. Entire swimming pools, floating like giant bubbles. | A pool's water floating over the city as a huge blue bubble |
| 10 | Last year, our mailman let go to scratch his nose. | Mailman scratching his nose, letters flying up |
| 11 | We still see him sometimes. He waves. | Tiny mailman high in the sky, waving |
| 12 | But me? My name is Theo, and I have a secret. | Medium shot: Theo (green hoodie) facing camera |
| 13 | When gravity turns off… I don't float. | Face close-up: Theo, nervous half-smile |
| 14 | Not even a little. | Theo standing normally while everything floats around him |
| 15 | The first time, I was seven. | Flashback: little Theo at the breakfast table |
| 16 | My whole family was hanging on for their lives, and I was just sitting there, eating cereal. | Family clinging to furniture, cereal floating; little Theo still eating |
| 17 | My mom grabbed my face and whispered, "Theo, nobody can ever know." | Close-up: Mom's worried face |
| 18 | So every day at 11:59, I pretend. | Theo grabbing a pole with both arms |
| 19 | I hold a pole. I fake-scream. | Theo fake-screaming, one eye open |
| 20 | I even practice my floating face in the mirror. | Theo making a dramatic face in a bathroom mirror |
| 21 | Once, I forgot. Everyone floated up, and I was the only kid still sitting at my desk. | Classroom: whole class floating; Theo alone in his seat |
| 22 | So I jumped on my desk and flapped my arms like a bird. | Theo on his desk flapping his arms |
| 23 | Nobody noticed. I think. | Face close-up: Theo, side-eye at the camera |
| 24 | It worked for six years. Until today. | Face close-up: Theo's expression drops |
| 25 | At 11:58, my best friend Maya climbed onto the school roof to catch her runaway hamster. | Maya on the roof reaching for a hamster |
| 26 | 11:59. Everyone grabbed on. | School clock at 11:59; students grabbing railings |
| 27 | Maya didn't. | Maya still chasing the hamster |
| 28 | 12:00. | Clock hits 12:00 |
| 29 | Her feet left the roof. | Close-up: Maya's sneakers lifting off |
| 30 | She reached for the railing, missed, and started drifting up. | Maya's hand missing the rail |
| 31 | Ten feet. Twenty feet. | Low angle: Maya rising against the sky; in-world "20 FT" sign |
| 32 | Everyone screamed, but no one could let go. | Students clinging and screaming |
| 33 | No one… except me. | Extreme close-up: Theo's eyes, determined |
| 34 | I let go of my pole. | Theo's hand releasing the pole |
| 35 | I sprinted across the playground while the whole school floated around me like confetti. | Tracking shot: Theo running, backpacks and kids floating |
| 36 | I climbed the fire ladder, jumped, and grabbed her ankle. | Theo grabbing Maya's ankle midair |
| 37 | Got you! | Close-up: Theo grinning |
| 38 | Then gravity came back. | Everything slams down; Theo and Maya land on the roof |
| 39 | That's when I noticed him. | Theo looking down toward the gate |
| 40 | A man in a black suit, standing perfectly still beside a black van. | Wide: man in a black suit by a black van at the school gate |
| 41 | Everyone else had been holding on. He hadn't. | Close-up: his shoes, planted on the ground, no rope, no railing |
| 42 | He looked straight at me, smiled, and said, "Found one." | Close-up: the man's smile. Cut on the last word |

**Alternative ending C (vote), replacing lines 39–42:**
> Then I heard a voice behind me. "Theo… how were you not floating?" It was Maya. Now she knows. What should I do, guys? Should I tell her the truth, or say she imagined it? Vote in the poll below. Whichever gets the most votes, that's what I'll do in part two.

## Premise ideas (original)

1. In this town, every lie you tell makes you one inch shorter.
2. In this town, your shadow is a separate person, and mine just quit.
3. In this town, the weather changes with the mayor's mood.
4. In this town, everyone gets one superpower on their 13th birthday. Mine is useless… or is it?
5. In this town, if you fall asleep before midnight, you wake up in someone else's body.
6. In this town, every house has a door to the future, and nobody is allowed to open it.
7. In this town, your pet becomes your boss when you turn 12.
8. In this town, money grows on trees, but only for people who have never cheated.
9. In this town, you can trade one year of your life for anything you want.
10. In this town, the sun only rises if someone tells the mayor a joke he's never heard.
